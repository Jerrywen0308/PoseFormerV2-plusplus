Official repository for our research on 3D Human Pose Estimation, accepted by the Asian Conference on Computer Vision (ACCV 2026).

## Overview

This work investigates frequency-domain feature modeling and graph-based spatial representation learning for 3D human pose estimation.

Our approach incorporates two key components:

- **FreqSA:** Frequency-aware feature modeling for temporal representations.
- **NGRGConv:** Graph-based modeling to enhance spatial relationships among human joints.

The proposed approach aims to improve 3D pose estimation accuracy through more effective spatial and temporal feature representations.
